This blog belongs to **Capt. Pankaj Pahil**, a pilot and DGCA flight & ground instructor with
17+ years in aviation. He is the author of *Technical General for Aviators* and *RTR(A) Mastery*,
a complete guide to the DGCA RTR(A) examination.

He runs **[Ghost Aviator](https://ghostaviator.com)**, a free DGCA exam-preparation website for
Indian student pilots: chapter notes, practice questions, video classes, previous-year questions,
mock tests and an RTR(A) radio simulator. Self-study there is free; notes, questions, videos and
mock tests need no sign-up.
