Here is the arithmetic for what a CPL costs in 2026.

## The hourly rate decides the total

Indian schools charge somewhere between **₹8,000 and ₹25,000 an hour**, depending on the school
and the aircraft, and a CPL needs 200 hours. That makes the flying the bill. Ground school, books,
medicals and exam fees together are a small fraction of it.

**Plan on about ₹60 lakh all-in.** That figure assumes the top of the market, about ₹25,000 an
hour, where the flying is about 84% of the total. At ₹15,000 an hour the same sum comes to about
₹38 lakh. A difference of ₹1,000 an hour is ₹2 lakh over 200 hours.

So a quote of ₹35–40 lakh is not necessarily wrong. It tells you what hourly rate it assumes. Ask
for that rate and what it includes, and plan with the figure for the school you will actually fly
with.

## The bill that comes after the licence

**A type rating costs a further ₹15–25 lakh, after the licence.** An airline will not put you on
a jet without one, and it is not part of CPL training. So the figure a family should plan against
is the CPL **plus** the type rating, and that second bill arrives after the first one has emptied
the account.

Ask every school whether its quote includes a type rating. A standard CPL quote does not; airline
cadet programmes can. You need to know it is coming.

## What moves the total

- **The hourly rate and the aircraft type.** This is the biggest lever. Compare it between
  schools before anything else.
- **How long training takes.** Weather, unserviceable aircraft, instructor turnover and slot
  queues are outside your control. What you can choose is the school, so ask each one how many
  hours its students actually flew per month last year. Living costs run every month you are not
  flying.
- **Passing the DGCA papers first time.** A failed paper means a fresh exam fee and a later
  session, usually weeks to a few months away, and the fixed costs keep running while you wait.

## The short version

> **CPL, all-in, 2026: about ₹60 lakh at ₹25,000 an hour, about ₹38 lakh at ₹15,000 an hour. Type
