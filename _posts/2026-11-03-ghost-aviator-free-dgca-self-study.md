**[Ghost Aviator](https://ghostaviator.com) is a free website for Indian student pilots preparing
for the DGCA CPL written exams, with a shared study library for the three ATPL general papers.**
It was built by Capt. Pankaj Pahil, a pilot and DGCA flight &amp; ground instructor with 17+ years
in aviation. Self-study on it is free: notes, questions, videos and mock tests need no sign-up. In
the RTR(A) simulator the first scenario needs no account, and the other 18 open with a free
sign-in.

## Why it exists

Ground training for the DGCA papers can cost lakhs of rupees. A second cost is easier to miss: a
wrong answer learnt from an unchecked question bank can cost a student a paper. That means a fresh
exam fee and a later session, usually weeks to a few months away, while living costs keep running.
Ghost Aviator was built to take the first cost to zero and to make the second one rarer.

## What is on it

The figures below were read from the live site on 3 November 2026.

- **Chapter notes**: 242 published chapters across the CPL syllabus: Air Navigation, Instruments
  and Radio Navigation, Meteorology, Air Regulations, Human Performance, Technical General, Radio
  Telephony and the DA42 type notes. ([All notes](https://ghostaviator.com/notes))
- **Over 5,200 practice questions** with the answer marked; about 3,600 of them also carry a
  written explanation. ([Question bank](https://ghostaviator.com/question-bank))
- **272 free video classes**, organised by subject and chapter.
  ([Video lectures](https://ghostaviator.com/video-lectures))
- **1,805 questions recalled by candidates after DGCA sittings** (a few rebuilt from a recalled
  topic) in Meteorology, Air Regulations and Navigation, sorted by topic. Almost every one lists
  the sittings it came up in, so you can see which topics keep coming back.
  ([Previous-year questions](https://ghostaviator.com/dgca-exam-questions))
- **Timed mock tests** in the DGCA paper format. ([Exam mode](https://ghostaviator.com/exam))
- **An RTR(A) radio simulator**, where you speak to a simulated ATC unit, read back clearances
  and get graded. ([RTR(A) simulator](https://ghostaviator.com/rtr-simulator))
- **Guides** to the paperwork side of becoming a pilot: the DGCA computer number, the CPL exam
  pattern, what training costs, and the whole path from medical to licence.
  ([Guides](https://ghostaviator.com/guides))

## How the answers are checked

The rule is that an answer is checked against an authoritative reference (an ICAO Annex, the
relevant DGCA CAR, or a standard ATPL textbook), and anything that cannot be checked is flagged or
removed rather than published as a guess.

The site also says plainly where that work is unfinished. The audit status of the Meteorology, Air
Regulations, Air Navigation and DA42 material is published at
[How answers are verified](https://ghostaviator.com/how-answers-are-verified), which also says that
the other subjects have not yet had a formal audit.

## Four facts to check your notes against

- **A CPL or ATPL in India is valid for ten years**, not five. The change came with G.S.R. 733(E)
  of 10 October 2023, which amended Rule 39C of the Aircraft Rules, 1937.
- **There is no negative marking in the DGCA papers.** A blank is a certain zero, so attempt every
  question.
- **The pass mark is 70% in each paper.** Marks are not averaged across papers.
- **The RTR(A) exam is conducted by the DGCA**, which took it over from the WPC. Its CAR, Section 7
  Series G Part VI, took effect on 1 December 2025.

More short answers like these are on the [DGCA exam FAQ](https://ghostaviator.com/faq).

## Where to start

If you have just begun, read
[How to become a pilot in India](https://ghostaviator.com/guides/how-to-become-a-pilot-in-india),
then [the CPL exam pattern](https://ghostaviator.com/guides/dgca-cpl-exam-pattern). If you have a
paper coming up, open that subject's notes next to its recalled questions (Meteorology, Air
Regulations, Navigation), or sit a full-length paper on
[Past papers](https://ghostaviator.com/past-papers) (previous-year papers for Air Regulations,
sample papers for the other subjects).
