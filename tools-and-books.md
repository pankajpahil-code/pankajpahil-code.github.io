## 3. DGCA CPL Subject Study Hubs

- **[Air Regulations](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/air-regulations)** — ICAO Annexes, Chicago Convention, Indian Aircraft Act 1934, Aircraft Rules 1937, DGCA CARs, Air Traffic Services, PANS-OPS, and licensing.
- **[Aviation Meteorology](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/meteorology)** — Atmosphere, ISA, pressure systems, altimetry, winds, thermodynamics, clouds, icing, thunderstorms, Indian climatology, and METAR/TAF/SIGWX.
- **[Air Navigation](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/air-navigation)** — Earth geometry, 1-in-60 rule, charts (Mercator, Lambert, Polar Stereographic), time, Critical Point (CP) & Point of No Return (PNR), Mass & Balance, Performance, and CX-3 flight computer methods.
- **[Human Performance & Limitations](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/human-performance)** — Aviation physiology (hypoxia, TUC, decompression, vision, vestibular system, spatial disorientation) and aviation psychology (information processing, CRM, fatigue, stress, and sleep).
- **[Radio Navigation](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/radio-navigation)** — Radio propagation, NDB/ADF, VOR/DVOR, ILS, MLS, DME, primary & secondary surveillance radar (SSR), RNAV, FMS, and GNSS/GPS.
- **[Radio Telephony — RTR(A)](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/radio-telephony)** — Complete DGCA RTR(A) written and practical phraseology, emergency procedures, propagation, and aeronautical communications.
- **[Aircraft & Engines — Technical General](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/technical-general)** & **[Flight Instrumentation](https://ghostaviator.com/cpl/instrumentation)** — Pitot-static instruments, gyros, magnetism, INS/IRS, EFIS/FMS, aerodynamics, piston & gas turbine engines, and aircraft systems.
